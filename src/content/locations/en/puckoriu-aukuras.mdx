The Pūčkoriai Hillfort Fire Altar (Pūčkorių piliakalnio aukuras) is a contemporary sacred site located on top of the ancient Pūčkoriai Hillfort in the Pavilniai Regional Park, Vilnius. The hillfort itself was a fortified Baltic settlement dating from the 1st millennium BC to the 14th century AD.

A modern stone fire altar (aukuras) was constructed on the hillfort by Baltic neopagans (Romuva). During seasonal festivals such as the equinoxes and solstices, the community gathers here to kindle the sacred fire, perform rituals to the goddess Gabija, sing traditional dainas, and leave symbolic offerings of amber dust, grain, and mead, maintaining a living bridge to the ancient Baltic spiritual heritage.
