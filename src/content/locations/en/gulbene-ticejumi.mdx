Gulbene in north-east Vidzeme appears in Šmits *ticējumi* entries on sumpurņi (LTT ~28992–28993; J. Banga). It is the closest major town to the Alūksne area with attested dogsnout belief texts.

Treat Gulbene as a collection provenance for forest man-eater lore, not as a traditional cult lair or pilgrimage site.
