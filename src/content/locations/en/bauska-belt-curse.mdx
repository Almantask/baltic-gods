The Bauska district in Semigallia is linked to Šmits belief text LTT ~3902 (G. Pols): sorcerers could turn people into *sumpurņi* or *vilkači* by girding them with a belt.

The enchantment ends only when the belt is untied or torn apart. Bauska thus documents the werewolf-overlap transformation rite for dogsnouts, not a physical cult altar.
