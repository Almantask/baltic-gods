The Vido Stone (Vido akmuo, also called Pūčkorių akmuo) is a massive granite gneiss boulder located on the edge of the Pūčkoriai forest in the Pavilniai Regional Park, Vilnius. It is a prominent glacial erratic that was officially registered as a state protected geological monument in 2022.

The stone is split vertically down the middle into two distinct halves. While it does not bear ancient, pre-Christian mythological names in historical records, it is the most significant natural boulder in the immediate Vilnia river valley, standing as a silent, physical witness to the region's glacial past and natural heritage.
