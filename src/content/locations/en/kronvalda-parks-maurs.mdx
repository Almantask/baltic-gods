Kronvalda Park in Riga holds Rihards Maurs’s 1933 artificial-stone fountain group *Sumpurnis*, *Bārenīte*, and *Mātesmeita*. The sculptures stage a fairy-tale encounter rather than an ethnographic cult image.

They mark twentieth-century urban reception of the dogsnout figure in Latvian public art, paired with the orphan / witch-daughter tale cycle that also feeds the *sumpurņa pils* carnival title.
