Vecpiebalga in Vidzeme is the recording place of *Cilvēku bērni pie sumpurņiem* (ATU 327A; LP VII, I, 998). In the tale, sumpurņi fatten an abducted girl on nut kernels and sweet milk, pierce her forehead to lick the blood, and prepare an oven for three days.

She escapes by shoving the *sumpurniene* into the oven and fleeing in ash-filled shoes worn backwards (*purnu papēdī*) so the dog-scent trail is lost. The parish is a folklore provenance for the oven-escape cycle, not a sacred cult site.
