## 1. Epic Origin and Mythological Attributes

Lāčplēsis ("Bear-Slayer") is Latvia's primary national hero, codified by Andrejs Pumpurs in his 1888 epic *Lāčplēsis: Latvju tautas eposs*. Born of a human mother and a bear, his supernatural strength resided in his bear ears, which allowed him to tear open a bear's jaws with his bare hands.

## 2. Defence of the Homeland and Epic Destiny

Lāčplēsis defended Castle Lielvārde and the Daugava River valley against dark forces, witches, and foreign crusaders, until the traitor Kangars betrayed his secret to the Black Knight (*Melnais bruņinieks*).
