## 1. Primary Chronicle Records

Lizdeika is the legendary 14th-century High Priest (*Vyriausiasis žynys / Krivis*) of Vilnius. The *Chronicle of Bychowiec* records that he was found as an infant in an eagle's nest (*lizdas*) by Grand Duke Vytenis or Gediminas. Maciej Stryjkowski (*Kronika*, 1582) details Lizdeika's famous interpretation of Gediminas' dream of the Iron Wolf on Tauro Hill.

## 2. Legendary Prophecy and Cultural Role

When Gediminas dreamt of a howling iron wolf on the hill, Lizdeika declared that the wolf represented an impregnable capital city whose fame would resound across the world, leading to the founding of Vilnius in 1323.
