## 1. Chronicle Records and Sacred Geography

Šventaragis is the legendary Lithuanian Duke recorded in the *Chronicle of Bychowiec* who founded the sacred cremation valley (*Šventaragio slėnis*) at the confluence of the Neris and Vilnia rivers in Vilnius. He decreed that all future Grand Dukes of Lithuania be cremated there alongside their horses and weapons.

## 2. Archaeological and Historical Significance

Dr. Vykintas Vaitkevičius (*Senosios Lietuvos šventvietės*, 2006) confirms that the Vilnius Cathedral Square site was an archaic pagan ritual fire and burial landscape.
