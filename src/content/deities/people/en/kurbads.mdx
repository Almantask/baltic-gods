## 1. Folktale Origins and Superhuman Feats

Kurbads is a fairytale hero recorded by Ansis Lerhis-Puškaitis (*Latviešu tautas pasakas*, 1891) in the Džūkste–Pienava area. Born miraculously after a mare ate a magical fish, Kurbads grew to adulthood in nine days and wielded a heavy iron club to defeat multi-headed monsters and subterranean devils.

## 2. The Sumpurnis Duel

In the underworld episode of the cycle, Kurbads frees a blinded herdsman by seizing a meadow-owning Sumpurnis by the dog throat, beating it with his club, and reclaiming a healing salve—while also shielding the monster's guardian-bird nestlings from hail. Rihards Zariņš later etched the fight as *Kurbads un sumpurnis* (1908–1911).
