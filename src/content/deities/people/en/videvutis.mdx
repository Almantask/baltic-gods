## 1. Chronicle Records and Prussian Foundation Myth

Videvutis (Widowuto) is the mythic 6th-century King of Old Prussia recorded by Dominican chronicler Simon Grunau (1526). According to the legend, Videvutis unified the twelve Prussian tribes, promulgated tribal laws, and established the sanctuary of Romove in Nadrovia. At the end of his life, he and his brother Prutenis ritually immolated themselves in a sacred oak pyre.

## 2. Academic Evaluation

Scholars Wilhelm Mannhardt (*Letto-Preussische Götterlehre*, 1936) and Vladimir Toporov identified Videvutis as a 16th-century chronicler foundation hero combining archaic Prussian ancestral traditions with Renaissance historiography.
