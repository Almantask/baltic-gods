## 1. Historical Chronicle Record

Herkus Monte (Henricus Monte / Herkus Mantas) is the historical Natangian Prussian leader documented by Peter von Dusburg in *Chronicon Terrae Prussiae* (1326). Educated in Magdeburg, Herkus Monte returned to Prussia and commanded the Great Prussian Insurrection (1260–1274) against the Teutonic Knights, scoring major victories at Pokarvis (1261) and Lubawa (1263).

## 2. Heroic Mythologization

In Baltic literature and cultural memory, Herkus Monte symbolizes unyielding resistance against feudal conquest and the defense of indigenous Baltic faith and freedom.
