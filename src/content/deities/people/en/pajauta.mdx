## 1. Chronicle Records and Legend

Pajauta is a legendary 13th-century Lithuanian duchess recorded in the *Chronicle of Bychowiec* and Maciej Stryjkowski's 1582 *Kronika*. Following her death in Kernavė, her son Prince Kukovaitis erected a carved wooden idol honoring her memory in Pajauta Valley.

## 2. Cultural Legacy

Pajauta Valley below the Kernavė hillforts remains one of Lithuania's most celebrated archaeological and legend landscapes.
