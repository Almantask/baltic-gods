## 1. Chronicle Record and High Priest Tradition

Prutenis (Brutenus) is the mythic first High Priest (*Crywe / Krivis*) of Old Prussia recorded by Simon Grunau (1526). The brother of King Videvutis, Prutenis founded the central sanctuary of Romove in Nadrovia and established the high priesthood (*Kriviai*) serving the divine triad Patolls, Patrimpas, and Perkūnas.

## 2. Academic Evaluation

Scholars analyze Prutenis and Videvutis as twin-hero founders (*Dioscuri* motif) representing sacred spiritual power (*Krivis*) and temporal military sovereignty (*King*).
