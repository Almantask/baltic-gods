## 1. Epic Character and Dual Transformation

Spīdola is a prominent sorceress in Latvian epic literature (Pumpurs 1888, Rainis 1905). Daughter of the sage Burtnieks, she initially opposes Lāčplēsis by flying through the night sky on a dragon. In Jānis Rainis' play *Uguns un nakts* (1905), Spīdola evolves into a symbol of spiritual transformation, light, and national rebirth.
