## 1. Historical Event and Legend

The Rose of Turaida (Maija Greif, 1601–1620) is Latvia's most famous romantic historical heroine. Discovered as an orphan infant after the 1601 Siege of Turaida Castle, she grew up near Gutmanis Cave. In 1620, ambushed by a deserter soldier in Gutmanis Cave, Maija sacrificed her life to protect her maidenly virtue and fidelity to her betrothed Victor.

## 2. Court Record Documentation and Legacy

1620 Vidzeme court records (*Turaidas Rozes tiesas protokoli*) confirm the historic trial of the murderer. Her grave beneath the ancient linden tree at Turaida Castle remains a beloved national shrine.
