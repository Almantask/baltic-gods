## 1. Primary Chronicle Records

Namejs (Nameisis) was the 13th-century Duke of Semigallia documented extensively in the *Livonian Rhymed Chronicle* (*Atskaņu hronika*). He commanded the Semigallian resistance against the Teutonic Knights (1279–1281), repelling sieges at Tērvete and joining forces with Grand Duke Traidenis of Lithuania.

## 2. The Namejs Ring Symbolism

The braided silver Namejs ring (*Nameja gredzens*) symbolizes unity, freedom, and Semigallian identity in modern Latvian culture.
