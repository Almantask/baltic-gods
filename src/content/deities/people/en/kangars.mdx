## 1. Epic Role and Cultural Symbolism

Kangars is the archetypal traitor priest in Andrejs Pumpurs' 1888 epic *Lāčplēsis*. Driven by jealousy, Kangars betrays the secret of Lāčplēsis' strength to the Crusader knights, becoming in Latvian culture the universal symbol for treason (*kangars*).
