## 1. Mythological Character and Disorientation Lore

Vadātājs is a Latvian disorienting spirit or ghost who leads travelers astray in dark forests, peat bogs, or at crossroads. Those under Vadātājs' spell find themselves walking in circles despite familiar landmarks.

## 2. Superstitions and Counter-Spells

Folk beliefs (*ticējumi*) compiled by Pēteris Šmits dictate that a lost traveler must stop, turn their coat inside out (*apgriezt svārkus uz otru pusi*), and change shoe direction to break Vadātājs' spell.
