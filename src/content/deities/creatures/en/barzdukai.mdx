## 1. Primary Chronicle Records and Folklore Definition

Barzdukai (Barstukai) are Old Prussian and Lithuanian subterranean dwarves living beneath elderberry trees (*sambucus*). First recorded in the 1561 *Sudovian Book* and Jan Łasicki's 1582 *De Diis Samagitarum*, they serve the earth god Puškaitis by secretly bringing grain and food abundance to kind, industrious peasants.

## 2. Academic Analysis

Lithuanian mythologist Norbertas Vėlius (*Mitinės būtybės: kaukai, aitvarai*, 1977) demonstrated that Barzdukai and Kaukai belong to the archaic Baltic subterranean wealth-bringing spirit layer.

## 3. Academic References
