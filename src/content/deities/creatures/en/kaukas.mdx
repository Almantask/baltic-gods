## 1. Chronicle Records and Household Lore

Kaukas (plural: Kaukai) is the subterranean household earth dwarf in Baltic folklore. First recorded by Martynas Mažvydas in his 1547 *Catechism* and Jan Łasicki in 1582 (*De Diis Samagitarum*), Kaukai dwell under stove thresholds and granary floors. When fed milk and presented with tiny woven linen shirts, Kaukai bring grain and prosperity to respectful farmsteads.
