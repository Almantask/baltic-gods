## 1. Primary Chronicle Record and Dragon Lore

Pūķis is the flying dragon and treasure-bringing spirit of Latvian folklore recorded by Lutheran pastor Paul Einhorn in his 1649 *Historia Lettica*. Raised from an egg laid by a nine-year-old rooster (*gaidņa ola*) or acquired through secret rituals, Pūķis flies as a trail of sparks through the night sky to bring stolen grain, money, and butter to his master's granary.

## 2. Offerings and Ritual Covenants

Farmers maintained Pūķis by leaving cooked meals and fried eggs (*pantāgs*) in the granary corner.
