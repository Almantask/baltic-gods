## 1. Chronicle Records and Water Dragon Lore

Smok (Smakas) is a amphibious lake serpent dragon in Old Prussian folklore recorded by Matthäus Praetorius (1690). Dwelling in deep bogs and marshy lakes in Pomesania and Pogesania, Smok guards underground gold hoards.
