## 1. Primordial Giants and Landscape Creation

Milžinai (Latvian: Milži) are the mythic primordial giants of Baltic folklore. Legends across Lithuania and Latvia describe how giants carried soil in their aprons to heap up hillforts (*piliakalniai*) and dug out river valleys with their giant wooden plows.
