## 1. Mythological Concept and Ancestral Cult

Vėlės (Latvian: Veļi) are the ancestral spirits and shade souls of deceased human beings in Baltic worldview. During the autumn soul-feasts (*Vėlinės / Veļu laiks*), descendants lit candles and prepared ceremonial meals for their returning ancestral shades.
