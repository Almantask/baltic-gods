## 1. Chronicle Record and Earth Wights

Markopoles (Marcopoles) are noble subterranean earth spirits of Old Prussian mythology documented in Matthäus Praetorius' 1690 manuscript *Deliciae Prussicae*. Residing under large, sacred field stones, Markopoles protected land boundaries and rewarded herders who poured milk offerings over their stone dwellings.

## 2. Academic References

- Matthäus Praetorius: *Deliciae Prussicae* (ms. c. 1690).
- Mannhardt, Wilhelm: *Letto-Preussische Götterlehre* (1936).
