## 1. Earliest Documented Baltic Deity (1249 Treaty of Christburg)

Curche (Curcho / Kurke) is the earliest historically documented Old Prussian deity, recorded in the February 7, 1249 Treaty of Christburg (*Vertrag von Christburg*). The Teutonic Knights mandated that converted Prussian clans renounce and burn their annual straw-and-grain idol of Curche.
