## 1. Chronicle Records and Romove Triad

Patrimpas (Patrimpus) is the youthful, grain-crowned god of spring, rivers, and grain fertility in the Old Prussian Romove triad (Simon Grunau 1526). Depicted with ears of grain and a sacred grass snake (*žaltys*) coiled in a clay jar, he granted bumper harvests and freshwater abundance.

## 2. Academic References

- Simon Grunau: *Preussische Chronik* (c. 1526).
- *Sudovian Book* (*Sudauer Büchlein*, c. 1520–1561).
- Visuotinė lietuvių enciklopedija: Patrimpas (https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/patrimpas/).
