## 1. Chronicle Evidence & The Royal Pantheon (1252)

**Nunašalis** (also documented in manuscripts as *Nunadievis*, *Nonadievis*, or *Numadievis*) is an archaic 13th-century sacred title for the supreme deity, recorded at the top of the Lithuanian divine pantheon in the **Hypatian Codex** (Volhynian Chronicle, 1252 entry).

The chronicle describes how King Mindaugas of Lithuania, following his political baptism, maintained private pagan sacrifices to his ancestral deities. Nunašalis/Nunadievis is listed first among these sacred figures, alongside *Teliavelis* (the divine smith), *Diviriks* (Perkūnas as divine sovereign), *Medeina* (forest goddess), and the *Rabbit God* (*Zajacy boh*).

## 2. Etymological Reconstructions & Scholarly Models

Modern mythologists and Baltic philologists have proposed three major etymological models:

1. **Sovereign of Our Land (*Nuna-šalis*)**: Formed from Baltic *\*nūnai / \*mūsų* (our / present) and *šalis* (land, realm, country). In this interpretation, Nunašalis signifies "Lord of Our Native Realm" or "Sovereign of the Earthly Land," invoking the supreme sky god in his capacity as defender of the sovereign homeland.
2. **God of the House / Dynasty (*Numa-dievis*)**: Derived from Proto-Baltic *\*numas / namas* (house, dwelling, family hearth). Proponents (such as A. Mierzyński and A. J. Greimas) view the title as "Lord of the Household," protecting the royal lineage and domestic hearth.
3. **Present-Day Temporal Lord (*Nūnai-dievis*)**: Championed by Dainius Razauskas, linking the stem to the adverb *nūnai* ("now", "today"). This positions Nunašalis as the deity presiding over the present era and middle realm—the realm of living humans.

## 3. Taboo Euphemism for Dievas (*Tabuizacija*)

Scholars Norbertas Vėlius, Gintaras Beresnevičius, and Dainius Razauskas concur that Nunašalis is not an isolated minor deity, but a sacred taboo euphemism (*eufemizmas*) for **Dievas** (the Supreme Sky Father).

Under Baltic sacred tradition, pronouncing the true personal name of the highest sky deity was tabooed. Rulers and priests invoked functional epithets: *Andajus* for the distant, cosmic sky vault, and *Nunašalis* / *Nunadievis* for the deity's active presence in the domestic hearth and terrestrial realm.
