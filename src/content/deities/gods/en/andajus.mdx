## 1. Primary Chronicle Record (1252 Hypatian Codex)

Andajus is the sacred taboo epithet for the High Sky God (**Dievas**) recorded in the 1252 Hypatian Codex as invoked by King Mindaugas of Lithuania. Linguist Kazimieras Būga and mythologists Gintaras Beresnevičius and Dainius Razauskas demonstrated that *Andajus* stems from Proto-Baltic *\*an-deivas* ("the god on high"), functioning as a taboo euphemism to avoid pronouncing the true sacred name of Dievas.

## 2. Academic Evaluation

Linguistic analysis refutes 19th-century romantic claims that Andajus was a separate pagan deity. Rather, it represents Baltic sacred name-taboo practice (*tabuizacija*), where supreme sky deities are invoked via indirect honorific titles.
