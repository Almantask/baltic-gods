## 1. Sudovian Book Attestation

Swayxtix (Swaixteix / Žvaigždikas) is the Old Prussian god of celestial starlight, sunbeams, and light listed in the 1561 *Sudovian Book*.

## 2. Academic References

- *Sudovian Book* (*Sudauer Büchlein*, c. 1520–1561).
- Visuotinė lietuvių enciklopedija: Swayxtix (https://www.vle.lt).
