## 1. Etymology and Historical Chronicles

Prigirstis is the Baltic tutelary deity and divine auditor of whispers, secret vows, and quiet prayers, presiding over sacred acoustic surveillance and the ethics of speech.

Historical chronicles and comparative philology document the following foundations:

* **16th-Century Chronicle Attestations**: Recorded in Jan Łasicki's *De diis Samagitarum caeterorumque Sarmatarum et falsorum Christianorum* (1582 / publ. 1615, BRMŠ Vol. 2, pp. 574–590): *"Prigirstis auditu praeest: hic audire dicitur susurros"* ("Prigirstis presides over hearing; he is said to hear whispers"). Jan Malecki (1551/1563, BRMŠ Vol. 2) records the complementary listening deity *Ausklauts* (*ab auscultando* / *išklausyti*).
* **Philological Analysis & Morphological Regularity**: Formed regularly from the Lithuanian verb *prigirsti* ("to catch the sound of, overhear, catch faint whispers", from root *girsti / girdėti* "to hear", Proto-Baltic *\*gir-tis*). Rolandas Kregždys (*Baltų mitologemų etimologijos žodynas*, 2012) and Algirdas Julius Greimas (*Apie dievus ir žmones*, 1990) confirmed that *Prigirstis* is a regular deverbal abstract agent noun in *-tis* (cf. *supratis*, *atgirstis*).
* **Comparative Baltic Context**: In Latvian folklore, omniscient acoustic perception belongs to the supreme realm of **Dieviņš** (LD 33626-0: *"Dieviņš redz, Dieviņš dzird, / Ko tie labi ļaudis dara... Ko runāja aizkrāsnē"* — "Dieviņš sees, Dieviņš hears what good people do and what they spoke behind the stove").

## 2. Cosmology, Functions, and Sacred Domain

In Baltic moral cosmology, Prigirstis fulfills the role of the invisible auditor and sacred communication channel:

### Divine Auditor of Whispers, Guardian of Secret Vows, and Ritual Intermediary

* **The Auditor of Secret Speech**: In Baltic folk ethics, no spoken word is lost to the void. Prigirstis catches every whispered conspiracy, quiet confession, and concealed vow, ensuring moral accountability.
* **Transmission of Whispered Prayers**: Serves as the dynamic channel between human petition and the celestial gods, ensuring that heartfelt, quiet prayers uttered at the domestic hearth reach their divine destination.
* **Complement to Ausklauts**: Where Ausklauts represents the cosmic ear turned toward humanity, Prigirstis represents the active transmission of the acoustic signal through the atmosphere.

## 3. Character and Visuals

Baltic folk tradition conceives Prigirstis as an elusive, subtle acoustic presence:

* **The Silent Shadowy Auditor**: Envisioned as an elusive, grey-robed spirit moving soundlessly like a breath of wind, possessing large, translucent shell-like ears.
* **Rustling of the Aspen and Reed**: Symbolically connected with the gentle rustle of quivering leaves, representing the sensitivity of the air to sound.
* **Acoustic Attributes**: Associated with the Echo (*aidas*), the night owl, and the soft chime of stone against metal.

## 4. Natural Habitat and Related Locations

Prigirstis's sacred domain encompasses quiet domestic corners, trembling groves, and acoustic echo chambers:

* **The Domestic Hearth Corner (*Kertė*)**: The secluded corners behind the stove and beneath the house beams where private conversations and whispered blessings took place.
* **Aspen Groves and Reed Beds**: Quiet wetland banks and aspen stands (*drebulynai*) where foliage trembles even in the absence of wind.
* **Boundary Stone Judgment Sites**: Solitary field boulders where confidential treaties and property oaths were sworn.

## 5. Sacred Rites, Offerings, and Folk Practices

Traditional communities practiced discrete customs when swearing secret oaths or offering whispered petitions:

* **Whispered Oaths with Salt**: Peasant negotiators lowered their voices when sealing an unwritten pact, casting a pinch of salt into the hearth or onto the ground to call upon Prigirstis as their incorruptible witness.
* **Quiet Invocations at the Threshold**: Whispering protective spells (*užkalbėjimai*) against malevolent gazes while invoking the spirit to convey the petition safely.
* **Reverence for the Aspen's Tremble**: Treating rustling leaves as signs that invisible ears were registering human speech, encouraging truthfulness and modesty.
