## 1. Etymology and Historical Chronicles

Drebkulys (authentically recorded in 17th-century Baltic chronicles by Matthäus Praetorius as *Drebkulis* and defined by early lexicographers as *Erderschütterer* / "Earth-shaker") is the Baltic chthonic spirit presiding over earth tremors, deep subterranean rumblings, and tremors that shake hillfort ramparts.

Historical chronicles and philological studies establish his authentic textual lineage:

* **Matthäus Praetorius (*Deliciae Prussicae*, c. 1690)**: Provides the earliest authentic documentation of *Drebkulis*, describing him as a chthonic spirit of earth tremors and an attendant in the retinue of Pikulas (*Pykullis*), the sovereign of the underworld.
* **Jokūbas Brodovskis (*Lexicon Germanico-Lithuanicum*, c. 1740)**: Lexicographically defines *Drebkullis* as *Erderschütterer* (the shaker of the earth).
* **Philipp Ruhig (*Littauisch-Deutsches Lexicon*, 1747)**: Confirms the teonym as an earth-trembling entity active in Prussian-Lithuanian tradition.
* **Etymological and Philological Analysis**: The teonym derives from the Baltic verbal root *\*dreb-* (*drebėti* "to tremble, shake, vibrate") combined with the agentive suffix *-kulys* / *-kulis* (from *kulti* "to strike, pound, thrash"), signifying "the one who pounds and causes trembling."

The authentic history of Drebkulys is analysed by Norbertas Vėlius (*Baltų religijos ir mitologijos šaltiniai*, Vol. 3) and modern mythological studies.

## 2. Cosmology, Functions, and Sacred Domain

In Baltic chthonic cosmology, subterranean realms beneath geological fault lines and deep ravines were viewed as active, living domains governed by underworld forces.

### Sovereign of Geological Vibrations and Chthonic Tremors

* **Master of Subterranean Rumblings**: Governs mysterious underground noises, geological settling, and tremors that shake the foundations of hillforts.
* **Underworld Threshold Sentinel**: Serves in the retinue of Pikulas, guarding subterranean gateways, deep caverns, and root fissures where the upper earth touches the underworld.
* **Preserver of Chthonic Balance**: Releases underground pressure through fissures and faults, maintaining the dynamic tension between the surface earth and underworld depths.

## 3. Character and Visuals

Baltic folklore and historical descriptions portray Drebkulys through rugged chthonic iconography:

* **Subterranean Giant / Chthonic Elder**: Conceived as a heavy, low-statured earthen figure with dark ash-grey complexion and stone-textured skin.
* **Deep Granite Mallet**: Carries a massive stone-headed mallet or basalt club used to strike subterranean bedrock.
* **Root-Bound Mantle**: Clothed in a heavy garment woven from compressed peat, dark clay, and ancient subterranean tree roots.
* **Earth-Stained Beard**: Features a thick, dust-crusted beard resembling tangled root fiber and deep flint-grey eyes reflecting cavern darkness.

## 4. Natural Habitat and Related Locations

The sacred domain of Drebkulys is situated along deep river valley faults and chthonic sanctuaries:

* **Ragainė and Tilžė Ridges (Lower Nemunas)**: Steep geological fault bluffs and hillfort ravines historically associated with subterranean echoes.
* **Rambynas Hill Slopes**: Deep ravines along the Nemunas where chthonic subterranean spirits were venerated.
* **Underworld Fissures and Caves**: Subterranean hollows, deep sinkholes, and ancient spring fissures in the Prussian-Lithuanian borderlands.

## 5. Sacred Rites, Offerings, and Folk Practices

Communities living near steep hillforts and geological faults observed dedicated appeasement customs:

* **Chthonic Rock Offerings**: Placing dark stone markers or pouring libations into deep ground crevices to appease earth-trembling forces.
* **Hillfort Foundation Consecration**: Burying consecrated stones beneath earth ramparts during construction to protect fortifications against landslides and tremors.
* **Subterranean Silence Rites**: Maintaining reverence and avoiding shouting in deep ravines or caves where underground echoes were heard.
