## 1. Chronicle Records and Romove Triad

Patollo (Patolls / Pecols) is the pale, white-bearded underworld god in the Old Prussian divine triad recorded by Simon Grunau (1526). Depicted on the sacred Romove banner with three human skulls, Patollo rules over death, night, shadows, and the spirits of the ancestors.

## 2. Cult and Sacrificial Rites

High Priest Prutenis maintained horse and human skulls at Patollo's shrine in the dark hollow of the sacred oak at Romove.
