## 1. Etymology & Historical Chronicles

Puschkaitis (Old Prussian: *Puschkaitis / Puschkayts*; Lithuanian: *Puškaitis*; Latvian: *Puškaitis*) is the Old Prussian chthonic deity of the fertile earth, sacred trees, and subterranean wealth. The theonym is linguistically connected to the Baltic root *\*puschk-* (attested in Lithuanian *puškuoti* "to breathe deeply, to sprout into bloom", Latvian *pušķot* "to adorn with blossoms, burst into bloom", and Old Prussian *bezds* / *sambucus*).

Historical chronicles provide detailed accounts of his cult across Old Prussia, Sudovia, and Western Lithuania:

* **Sudovian Book (*Sudauer Büchlein*, c. 1520–1530)**: Earliest direct record, cataloguing *Puschkayts* as the sovereign god of the earth residing beneath the sacred elder tree (*sambucus*), commanding the subterranean spirit-dwellers known as *Markopolen* and *Parstucken*.
* **Jan Malecki (*Libellus de sacrificiis et idolatria veterum Borussorum*, 1551)** and **Jan Łasicki (*De diis Samagitarum...*, 1582)**: Describe Puschkaitis as the protector of sacred groves and trees, receiving food offerings beneath elder roots so he would incline the nobility spirit Markopol not to burden common people.
* **Lucas David (*Preussische Chronik*, 1583)**: Records that Puschkaitis dwells beneath elder trees and within exceptionally clean, secluded spaces in farmhouses and cellars.
* **Jonas Bretkūnas (*Chronicon Prussiae*, 1588)**: Defines *Puschkaitus* as the divine ruler over fruits, vegetation, and all types of grain.
* **Matthäus Praetorius (*Deliciae Prussicae*, c. 1690)**: Designates *Puszkaitis* among the sovereign senior deities, commanding the *Basdukkas* (bearded subterranean dwarfs) and receiving nighttime feast offerings beneath elder and juniper trees.
* **Maciej Stryjkowski (*Kronika Polska, Litewska...*, 1582)**: Notes the veneration of Puschkaitis across Prussia, Samogitia, Livonia, and Curonia.

## 2. Cosmology, Functions, & Sacred Domain

In Old Prussian cosmology, Puschkaitis presides over the fertile subterranean realm, the nourishment of plant roots, and the invisible spirit economy of the homestead.

### Chthonic Earth Sovereign & Ruler of Hearth Spirits

* **Commander of Subterranean Spirits**: Rules over the **Kaukai** (benevolent wealth-bringing earth spirits), **Markopoles** (*Markopolen*, subterranean dwellers), and **Barzdukai** (*Parstucken / Basdukkas*, bearded earth dwarfs).
* **Granary Wealth Allocator**: Directs his subordinate spirits to transport grain, butter, and agricultural bounty from greedy households into the granaries of generous, hospitable farmers.
* **Master of the Earth Axis**: Connects the underworld with the surface through the root systems of sacred trees, channeling subterranean moisture and nutrients into agricultural crops.
* **Allied Pantheon Position**: Operates alongside agricultural deities such as **Curcho** and earth guardians like **Žemėpatis**, bridging the wild forest groves and cultivated village farmsteads.

## 3. Character & Visuals

Historical sources and comparative Baltic mythography depict Puschkaitis as an imposing chthonic ruler:

* **Chthonic Sovereign Demeanor**: Visualized as a venerable, deep-voiced earth elder with a dense, moss-grey beard, sharp soil-dark eyes, and a weathered, earthy complexion.
* **Earthy Garments**: Clothed in heavy, homespun linen robes tinted in forest greens and bark brown, cinched with a woven hempen belt.
* **Emblematic Accessories**: Associated with polished stone bowls, miniature digging shovels, and earthen flagons of grain beer.
* **Accompaniment of Earth Dwarfs**: Surrounded by diminutive bearded spirits (*Barzdukai*) moving through root hollows and shadowed granary corners.

## 4. Natural Habitat & Related Locations

The sacred domain of Puschkaitis spans the ancient sacred groves and settlement trees of Old Prussia:

* **Sacred Elder Trees (*Sambucus nigra / Bezds*)**: Ancient elder bushes growing near farm boundaries and hillfort slopes, revered as the direct gateway to his subterranean court.
* **Juniper Groves (*Kadagynai*)**: Coniferous sanctuaries in Nadruvia, Skalva, and Samogitia identified by chroniclers as dwelling places of the deity.
* **Sambia Peninsula Sacred Sites**: Wooded settlements across Sambia and Natangia where elder root altars received continuous clan devotion.
* **Clean Farmstead Cellars & Granaries**: Dedicated domestic corners kept immaculate to welcome visits from Puschkaitis's spirit servants.

## 5. Sacred Rites, Offerings, & Folk Practices

Historical treatises document elaborate ritual banquets dedicated to Puschkaitis and his spirit host:

* **Nocturnal Elder Root Feasts (*Vaišės po šeivamedžiu*)**: Rural families gathered after nightfall, placing wooden tables or clean cloths beneath sacred elder trees bearing fresh bread, beer, cheese, and hot porridge.
* **Granary Tables for Barzdukai**: Setting a lavish table in the grain barn overnight; if the food was consumed by dawn, it was received as a sign of Puschkaitis's favor and coming prosperity.
* **Priestly Supplications**: Tribal priests (*waidler*) and diviners (*zegnoten*) invoked Puschkaitis at boundary stones to protect borders and recover stolen livestock.
* **Sanctuary Purity Taboo**: Chroniclers emphasize that any location associated with Puschkaitis had to remain spotlessly clean; disturbing sacred elder boughs was considered a grave sacrilege.
