## 1. Sudovian Book Hierarchy

Okopirmus (Occopirnys / Ukapirmas) heads the Old Prussian pantheon in the 1561 *Sudovian Book*. Meaning "The Absolute First", he is the supreme sky father corresponding to Proto-Baltic *\*Deiuas*.
