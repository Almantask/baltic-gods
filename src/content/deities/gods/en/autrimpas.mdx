## 1. Chronicle Record and Sea Cult

Autrimpas is the Old Prussian god of the Baltic Sea, sea waves, and maritime navigation listed in the 1561 *Sudovian Book*. Coastal fishermen offered him raw amber and beer for smooth sailing.

## 2. Academic References

- *Sudovian Book* (*Sudauer Büchlein*, c. 1520–1561).
- Visuotinė lietuvių enciklopedija: Autrimpas (https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/autrimpas/).
