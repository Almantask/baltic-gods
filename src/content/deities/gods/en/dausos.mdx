## 1. Etymology and Historical Chronicles

Dausos represents the Baltic personification of the luminous afterlife paradise, the cosmic mountain of souls, and the celestial dwelling of the departed.

Historical traditions and Baltic linguistics establish the following foundations:

* **Eschatological Studies**: Researched extensively in Baltic mythological studies and Gintaras Beresnevičius's foundational monograph *Dausos: pomirtinio gyvenimo samprata senojoje lietuvių pasaulėžiūroje* (1990), which analyzed the archaic structure of the Baltic heaven as a luminous mountain realm.
* **Etymology & Cosmic Semantics**: Derived from Proto-Baltic *\*daus-* (related to Lithuanian *daũsos*, *dvasia* "spirit/soul", *dūsti / dūsauti* "to breathe/gasp", *dvelkti*, and dialectal *dausa* "warm southern breeze / winter refuge of birds", Latvian *dvaša*, from PIE *\*dheu-s-* "breath, spirit, vitality"). Signifies a realm of gentle warmth, pure light, and breath of life.
* **Comparative Baltic Context**: In Latvian folklore, Dausos corresponds directly to **Aizsaule** ("beyond the sun") and **Viņsaule** ("that other sun / otherworld"), where the souls of ancestors (**veļi**) abide under the maternal care of **Veļu māte** (LD 27760-0, LD 27552-0).

## 2. Cosmology, Functions, and Sacred Domain

In Baltic eschatology, Dausos occupies the uppermost celestial sphere of the tripartite cosmic order:

### The Summit of the World Mountain and Luminous Abode of Vėlės

* **The Celestial Paradise**: Envisioned as a transcendent realm situated atop the cosmic mountain (in folklore, the steep Glass Mountain / *stiklo kalnas*) in the southwestern sky, characterized by eternal spring, golden light, and peaceful green meadows.
* **Cosmological Dualism**: Stands in contrast to the dark, subterranean, or watery domain of Velnias; Dausos is the celestial abode where righteous ancestral souls (*vėlės*) rest in warmth and harmony.
* **Avian Soul Conduit**: Connected to the earth via the Milky Way (*Paukščių Takas* / *Vėlių Kelias*), which serves both as the cosmic road for ascending souls and the seasonal skyway for migrating birds wintering in the warm celestial south.
* **Pantheon Relationships**: Governed in harmony with **Dievas** (the heavenly Sky God), **Perkūnas** (guardian of cosmic order), and **Vėjopatis** (ruler of winds and coastal breezes).

## 3. Character and Visuals

Baltic folklore depicts Dausos through luminous, crystal, and pastoral cosmic imagery:

* **The Radiant Mountain**: Manifests as a steep, shining Glass Mountain (*stiklo kalnas*) rising into the azure sky, surrounded by shimmering silver orchards and crystalline streams.
* **Pastoral Eternity**: Envisioned with blooming clover fields, golden apple trees, and gentle warm light devoid of freezing winter cold or scorching heat.
* **Sacred Amulets and Avian Emblems**: Accompanied by lynx claws (*lūšies nagai*), bear claws (*lokio nagai*), white swans (*gulbės*), and storks (*gandrai*).

## 4. Natural Habitat and Related Locations

Dausos's sacred geography spans celestial coordinates and earthly ritual gateways:

* **The Cosmic Mountain Summit**: The highest point of the upper sky where the sun pauses before dusk.
* **The Milky Way (*Paukščių Takas*)**: The luminous starry ribbon traversing the night sky.
* **Sacred Barrow Fields and Cremation Hills (*Pilkapynai / Alkakalniai*)**: Ancient earth mounds where funeral pyres released the soul into the rising smoke.

## 5. Sacred Rites, Offerings, and Cult Practices

Baltic communities observed solemn funeral and commemorative rites to aid souls on their journey to Dausos:

* **Burial Claw Amulets**: In archaic burial practices, mourners placed lynx and bear claws inside burial urns and graves, providing the departed *vėlė* with the claw-traction necessary to ascend the slippery glass mountain.
* **Funeral Pyre Ascent**: Cremation smoke was believed to carry the soul skyward into Dausos, accompanied by prayers for a gentle reception among the ancestors.
* **Autumn Feast of the Ancestors (*Vėlinės / Ilgės / Veļu laiks*)**: Families gathered in autumn to offer food, beer, honey, and warm hearth light, inviting the souls from Dausos to visit and bless the living.
