## 1. Etymology and Tradition

Kārta is the Baltic fate goddess of life thread duration and lot arrangement, worshipped in Kurzeme alongside Laima and Dēkla. Her name derives from the Latvian verb *kārtot* (to set in order, arrange) or *kārts* (loom pole/warp beam).

Haralds Biezais (*Die Hauptgöttinnen der alten Letten*, 1955) analyzed Kārta as the third member of the Baltic fate triad, comparing her role to the Scandinavian Norn Skuld or Greek Moira Clotho.

## 2. Cosmology and Rituals

Kārta arranges the decrees fixed by Laima at birth, laying the pillow (*"Kārta lika pagalviņu"*) and governing the span of human life.
