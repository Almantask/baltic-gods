## 1. Etymology and Historical Chronicles

Gulbi (recorded historically as *Gulbi dzievos*) is the Baltic tutelary guardian spirit (*genius bonus*), personal protector of the soul, and sacred avian avatar of divine grace.

Sixteenth-century chronicles and Baltic comparative folklore document this tutelary tradition:

* **Sixteenth-Century Chronicle Attestations**: Documented in Jan Łasicki's treatise *De diis Samagitarum caeterorumque Sarmatarum et falsorum Christianorum* (composed c. 1582, published Basel 1615, reprinted in BRMŠ Vol. 2): *Gulbi dzievos, uniuscuiusque genius bonus* ("Gulbi dzievos, the good genius [guardian spirit] of each individual"). Maciej Stryjkowski's *Kronika Polska...* (1582, BRMŠ Vol. 2) similarly describes *Gulbi dzievos* as each person's protective guardian angel (*anioł stróż abo duch dobry*).
* **Baltic Linguistic Etymology**: Philologically analyzed by Kazimieras Būga and Ernst Fraenkel from the Lithuanian verb *gelbėti* ("to rescue, help, save", nominal agent *gelbėtojas* "the helper/savior"), reinforced by the symbolic association with the sacred white swan (Lithuanian *gulbė / gulbis*, Latvian *gulbis*, Old Prussian *\*gulbis*, derived from Proto-Indo-European *\*ghel-* "to shine, gleam, be bright").
* **Comparative Folklore Parallels**: Corresponds in Latvian folk dainas (LD 7514-0, LD 8456-0) and Baltic folktales (Type ATU 400: Swan Maidens) to the luminous swan as a sacred totem of purity, solar messengers of **Saulė**, and the individual soul's personal allotment (**Dalia** / **Laima**).

## 2. Cosmology, Functions, and Sacred Domain

In Baltic spiritual ontology, Gulbi accompanies each human soul through life as a personal divine protector:

### Personal Guardian Spirit, Soul Guide, and Preserver of Life-Force

* **Personal Guardian Angel (*Genius Bonus*)**: Accompanies the individual from birth, averting fatal misfortunes, disease, and spiritual corruption.
* **Soul Guide Across Waters**: Acts as a psychopomp and spiritual guide across cosmic waters and threshold rivers separating life from the ancestral realm.
* **Embodiment of Innocence and Solar Purity**: Reflects the untarnished light of the upper sky vault, inspiring peaceful households and honest living.

## 3. Character and Visuals

Baltic folk tradition conceives Gulbi through luminous avian and angelic imagery:

* **Radiant White Swan**: Manifested as an immaculate, snow-white swan gliding gracefully over mirror-like forest lakes.
* **White Swan Feather (*Balta plunksna*)**: Symbolized by the pristine white feather kept as a personal protective talisman.
* **Luminous Protective Halo**: Envisioned as a gentle solar radiance enveloping a protected individual in moments of danger.

## 4. Natural Habitat and Related Locations

The sacred terrain associated with Gulbi encompasses quiet waters, sacred springs, and family hearths:

* **Sacred Swan Lakes and Wetlands**: Peaceful forest lakes and reed-fringed wetlands where swans nest undisturbed.
* **The Domestic Hearth Threshold**: The family table and hearth where personal guardian spirits were acknowledged during meals.
* **Cosmic Crossing Waters**: The mythic river of light across which the swan guides the purified human soul.

## 5. Sacred Rites, Offerings, and Folk Practices

Ethnographic records and folk traditions preserve established customs honoring the guardian spirit:

* **Strict Swan Taboo**: A universal Baltic folk prohibition against harming or hunting swans, believed to bring instant doom upon the perpetrator's family.
* **Lakeside Grain and Bread Offerings**: Casting crumbs of ceremonial bread or unground grain onto sacred lake waters to honor the swan spirit.
* **Personal Hearth Libations**: Spilling a drop of milk, beer, or fresh water at the start of a communal meal for one's personal *genius bonus*.
