## 1. Etymology and Historical Chronicles

Ugniažaltis (the Fiery Grass Snake / *Ugninis Žaltys*) is the mythic Baltic serpent combining the subterranean sacredness of the grass snake with the luminous power of celestial and hearth fire.

Historical chronicles and folkloric archives document this tradition:

* **Folkloric and Archival Attestations**: Extensively recorded in Jonas Basanavičius's folklore collections (*Lietuviškos pasakos yvairios*, 1898–1902; *Iš gyvenimo vėlių bei velnių*, 1903) and Lithuanian Folklore Archives (LTR) as a luminous serpent dwelling beneath hearthstones or flying through the night sky.
* **Sixteenth-Century Historical Context**: Jesuit mission reports (1583–1612, reprinted in BRMŠ Vol. 2) and Jan Łasicki's *De diis Samagitarum* (1582/1615) document the domestic veneration of sacred grass snakes (*szalti*) kept near the hearth and fed bowls of sweet milk.
* **Baltic Linguistic Etymology**: Compound theonym formed from Lithuanian *ugnis* ("fire", Latvian *uguns*, Proto-Indo-European *\*n̥gʷnís*) and *žaltys* ("grass snake", Latvian *zalktis*, derived from Proto-Baltic *\*žel-* "green, flourishing, shining").

## 2. Cosmology, Functions, and Sacred Domain

In Baltic cosmological thought, Ugniažaltis acts as a vertical mediator between distinct cosmic levels:

### Cosmic Mediator of Fire and Earth, Guardian of Hearth Embers

* **Cosmic Vertical Mediation**: Unites the subterranean/chthonic water realm of the earth serpent with the celestial atmospheric fire of **Perkūnas** (lightning) and **Gabija** (hearth flame).
* **Guardian of Sacred Domestic Embers**: Dwells beneath the hearthstones (*pelenė, židinys*), safeguarding the ancestral fire and buried family prosperity.
* **Atmospheric Flight Form of the Wealth-Bringer**: Manifests in Lithuanian folktales as the nocturnal flight form of the **Aitvaras** (or Latvian **Pūķis**), flying across the heavens as a streak of sparks to drop grain and gold down the chimney.

## 3. Character and Visuals

Baltic folk legends (*sakmės*) depict Ugniažaltis through luminous reptilian imagery:

* **Golden-Scaled Serpent**: Manifested as a radiant serpent with golden or coppery scales (*auksažvynis*), ember-glowing eyes, and a crown of flame.
* **Nocturnal Streak of Sparks**: Appears in flight as a glowing fireball or meteor-like ribbon of sparks trailing across the dark sky.
* **Quiet Hearth Guardian**: Appears in the domestic kitchen as a sleek, harmless grass snake with yellow or orange ear crescents.

## 4. Natural Habitat and Related Locations

The sacred terrain of Ugniažaltis spans the domestic hearth, threshing floor foundations, and ancient mounds:

* **Domestic Hearthstones and Fireplaces (*Pelenė ir krosnis*)**: The earthen foundation beneath the family hearth in traditional farmsteads (`kernave-fire-snake-hearth`).
* **Barn Foundations and Grain Stores**: Warm cavities beneath log threshing floors where protective spirits were harbored.
* **Sacred Barrows and Hearth Cult Sites**: Ancient earthen mounds associated with perpetual fire sanctuaries.

## 5. Sacred Rites, Offerings, and Folk Practices

Ethnographic collections and chronicle records describe established serpent and hearth customs:

* **Milk Libations at the Hearth**: Placing earthenware bowls of fresh, unskimmed sweet milk (*pienas*) near the fireplace and in stable corners.
* **Evening Hearth-Covering Rites**: Raking glowing coals together into a dome of white ash while reciting evening blessings to Gabija (*„Šventoji Gabija, būk rami žarijose“*).
* **Fire Purity Taboos**: Strict prohibitions against spitting into hearth coals, cursing near the fire, or harming domestic snakes.
