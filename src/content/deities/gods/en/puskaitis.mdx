## 1. Primary Chronicle Records

Puškaitis (Puschkaitis) is the Old Prussian subterranean earth god recorded in the *Sudovian Book* (c. 1520–1561), Jan Łasicki (1582), and Matthäus Praetorius' *Deliciae Prussicae* (c. 1690). He rules over subterranean spirits, earth dwarves (Kaukai and Barstukai), and woodland growth from his sanctuary beneath sacred elderberry bushes (*sambucus*).

## 2. Cult Rituals and Offerings

Prussian farmers erected stone altars beneath elderberry trees, leaving nightly offerings of porridge, beer, and bread to Puškaitis so he would command his Kaukai to bring grain abundance to their barns.

## 3. Academic References

- *Sudovian Book* (*Sudauer Büchlein*, c. 1520–1561).
- Jan Łasicki: *De Diis Samagitarum* (1582; publ. 1615).
- Matthäus Praetorius: *Deliciae Prussicae* (ms. c. 1690).
