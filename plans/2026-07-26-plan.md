# Evidence-First Baltic Mythology Corpus

Date: 2026-07-26

## Scope and completion rule

- Include deities, spirits, creatures, legendary people, ritual figures, regional names, and disputed or reconstructed figures; label attestation rather than silently excluding uncertain material.
- Treat prose tales, legends, chronicle episodes, daina/folk-song motifs, ritual narratives, and local variants as eligible stories.
- Separate **catalogued** from **publishable**: every candidate enters the research catalog, but unverified claims do not enter public prose.
- Include traceable references whenever factual information is presented. Prefer clickable links to stable archive records, DOI pages, institutional repositories, or authoritative source pages.
- Define “comprehensive” as source coverage, not a finite claim of completeness: every named item in the approved source corpus is catalogued, and a tradition/source lane reaches saturation after two sweeps yield no new unique candidates.

## 1. Reconcile the existing corpus

- Generate a machine-readable baseline from [src/content/deities/meta.ts](../src/content/deities/meta.ts), [src/content/stories/meta.ts](../src/content/stories/meta.ts), [src/content/locations/meta.ts](../src/content/locations/meta.ts), the 231 dossiers in [ai/research/translations](../ai/research/translations), and [ai/research/baltic_mythology_checklist.md](../ai/research/baltic_mythology_checklist.md).
- Canonicalize aliases and cognates before counting them; distinguish “same being across traditions” from “similar but separate figure.”
- Resolve current drift first: stale checklist/generated-index counts, missing māte MDX, orphan MDX, duplicate/variant identities, and broken story links such as `medeina` versus `medine`.
- Make the machine-readable catalog authoritative and generate the human checklist from it, with statuses: `candidate`, `researched`, `verified`, `translated`, `published`, `blocked`.

## 2. Add an evidence and variant model

- Extend [src/types/content.ts](../src/types/content.ts) while preserving the existing TypeScript + trilingual MDX architecture.
- Add controlled fields for tradition, region/tribe, content kind, aliases with source attestations, evidence status, earliest attestation, source citations, and explicit relationships.
- Structure citations with author, title, year, page or archive identifier, source type, language, and URL/DOI/permalink. Preserve a complete bibliographic citation when no online copy exists.
- Extend stories with narrative type, story-family ID, `variantOf`, motif/catalog IDs, participants with roles, source/collector/place/date, related stories, and ritual context. Keep variants distinct rather than blending them into an invented canonical retelling.
- Use claim-level evidence statuses such as `confirmed`, `disputed`, `reconstruction`, and `unverified`; retain the current `references` field during migration for compatibility.
- Keep creatures and persons in the existing pantheon route initially, but replace deity-only internal assumptions with a broader “being” taxonomy where needed.

## 3. Put integrity gates in place before bulk ingestion

- Add a content validator alongside [scripts/generate-content.ts](../scripts/generate-content.ts) and run it from [package.json](../package.json) and [.github/workflows/ci.yml](../.github/workflows/ci.yml).
- Fail on duplicate IDs, unresolved being/location/story links, missing EN/LT/LV fields or MDX, fewer than two usable references, missing claim-to-reference mappings, invalid evidence records, generated-index drift, and orphan files.
- Validate citation completeness and prefer stable direct links over search-result or home-page links; periodically report broken or redirected URLs without discarding valid print-only sources.
- Add content-integrity tests under [src/__tests__](../src/__tests__) while retaining lint, Jest, build, and Playwright checks.
- Make claim-ledger fact-checking a required exit gate in [ai/workflows/orchestrated-dev-pipeline.md](../ai/workflows/orchestrated-dev-pipeline.md), not an optional follow-up.

## 4. Run systematic discovery by source lane

- Upgrade [ai/agents/resources/sources.md](../ai/agents/resources/sources.md) into a tiered source register: primary/archive, peer-reviewed scholarship, national encyclopedia, reputable synthesis, discovery-only, and modern revival.
- Search in parallel by lane: Lithuanian archives/chronicles, Latvian archives and dainas, Old Prussian chronicles/scholarship, archaeology and local legends, and comparative Baltic studies.
- Record the original-language name/text identifier, archive or page reference, stable direct URL or DOI, access date, and a short evidentiary excerpt or navigation phrase for every candidate.
- Use tertiary/community material only to discover leads. Require stronger sources before publication, and never use modern revival or an LLM as sole evidence.

## 5. Harvest stories at variant level

- Extract each source into a story family plus independently sourced variants, including short motifs and ritual narratives—not only polished prose tales.
- Capture the fullest defensible detail: event sequence, characters and roles, place, tribe/region, collector/source, attestation date, motifs, symbols, ritual setting, alternate endings, and conflicts between versions.
- Publish a sourced synopsis and clearly separated variants. Attach references to the specific paragraph, section, or variant they support, with a linked bibliography on every page. Quote only public-domain or appropriately licensed texts; summarize and cite copyrighted scholarship or translations.
- Prioritize the large story gap first: audit the existing 16 stories, then process the ~100 checklist candidates, Latvian daina motifs, Old Prussian chronicle episodes, and newly discovered local legends.

## 6. Research and verification pipeline

- Reuse the parallel discovery pattern in [ai/workflows/parallel-research-workflow.md](../ai/workflows/parallel-research-workflow.md) and the rapid gathering instructions in [ai/skills/research/SKILL.md](../ai/skills/research/SKILL.md).
- For each candidate, create an atomic claim ledger and run the LT/LV/source-specific checks defined by [ai/skills/fact-check/SKILL.md](../ai/skills/fact-check/SKILL.md). Add a dedicated Old Prussian verification lane rather than treating LT/LV agreement as proof.
- Require at least two independent references overall and, where available, one native-language primary/archive source. A single-source item may remain catalogued and visibly disputed but cannot be presented as settled fact.
- Process reviewable batches: roughly 10–20 story families or 20–30 beings per wave. Separate researchers who gather claims from the verification pass that approves them.

## 7. Enrich and publish in waves

- Wave 1: audit and correct all existing public entries and cross-links.
- Wave 2: split and ingest the existing dossiers in [ai/research/translations](../ai/research/translations), prioritizing well-sourced but unpublished material.
- Wave 3: expand stories and creatures/personas, where current coverage is thinnest.
- Wave 4: deepen Old Prussian, regional/tribal, ritual, cosmological, and disputed/reconstructed material.
- For every published item, produce semantically equivalent EN/LT/LV metadata and MDX, preserve native names/diacritics, display evidence labels, variant provenance, and linked references alongside the information they support, regenerate indexes, and run the full test/build suite.

## 8. Measure quality and continue discovery

- Track coverage by tradition, tribe/region, content kind, narrative type, evidence status, language parity, and source lane.
- Required publication metrics: zero broken internal links/orphans, 100% mandatory metadata, 100% evidence labeling, 100% claim-to-source coverage for factual assertions, linked references wherever a stable online source exists, and trilingual parity.
- Publish a generated gap report after every wave and rerun discovery periodically; the corpus remains open-ended as archives and scholarship expose additional variants.

## Implementation checklist

- [ ] Reconcile production content, dossiers, and checklist into a canonical machine-readable catalog.
- [ ] Define evidence, alias, relationship, motif, and story-variant schemas.
- [ ] Add structured, preferably linked citations and render them beside the information they support.
- [ ] Add automated content validation and make fact-checking a publication gate.
- [ ] Run tiered parallel discovery across Lithuanian, Latvian, Old Prussian, regional, and comparative sources.
- [ ] Create persistent claim ledgers and independently verify each research batch.
- [ ] Extract story families and source-specific variants with full provenance and narrative detail.
- [ ] Audit existing entries, ingest dossiers, expand gaps, translate, and publish in reviewable waves.
- [ ] Generate quality and saturation reports after every wave and continue periodic discovery.

### Known integrity issues

- Eight `domain: "God"` records are metadata-only and have no deity MDX: `debess-mate`, `zvaigznes-mate`, `saules-mate`, `meness-mate`, `rita-mate`, `vakara-mate`, `nakts-mate`, and `dienas-mate`.
- `miglas-mate` and `uguns-mate` have MDX but no production metadata registration; they remain unchecked registration candidates.
- Story `medeina-and-the-sacred-hare` links `beings: ["medeina"]`, while the registered deity slug is `medine`.
- The old research checklist's implementation marks and totals are stale. It also names `jelgava-stone` and `belmonto-stone`, neither of which is a current `LocationMeta` ID; current metadata instead contains the distinct `eleja-stone`.
- `romuva-grove` is stored with `region: "Latvia"` and an inline “proxy for Prussia” comment even though its subject is Romuva/Old Prussia.
- Production taxonomy mixes beings, abstractions, realms, symbols, and collectives under `domain: "God"` (for example `dausos`, `bird-goddess`, and `sutartiniu-dievaiciai`); this appendix preserves source metadata rather than silently reclassifying it.
- Several master-checklist items and dossiers rely on broad “folk,” regional, or single-compiler attributions. They are retained for corpus exhaustiveness but marked `needs verification` or `disputed` where the repository itself exposes uncertainty. Claim-level source verification remains a publication gate.
- The comparative “Basic Myth” dossier is mapped to existing story `perkunas-and-the-devil`; the Sky Wedding cycle is mapped to existing `the-wedding-of-saule-and-menulis`. Form variants of already registered Aitvaras, Pūķis, Ragana, Vilkacis, and cross-language deity names were not duplicated as missing beings.
- Alias review maps `Ezeru māte` to production `ezeru-seimininke` (`Ezera māte`), `Goniglis` to production `ganiklis`, `Kapa tēvs` to production `duobkasis`, and `Meža vīrs` to production `girinis`; only the potentially distinct `Mežavecis` remains unchecked. The requested orphan `uguns-mate` remains a registration task even though “Uguns māte” is also Gabija's Latvian alt-name.
- Proto-Baltic reconstructions (`*Deiuas`, `*Perkūnas`, `*Saulē`, `*Velinas`) and proposed Old Prussian reflexes remain separate research records even where a Lithuanian or Latvian descendant is in production; they are historical-linguistic hypotheses, not duplicate spelling variants.
- Euphemisms and taboo substitutes in the master audit (for example names for Velnias, Giltinė, wolves, and bears) are treated as aliases, not separate beings; grouped dossier names were split when the dossier describes separate functions or persons.
- Modern revival organizations and modern scholars listed in the master audit were excluded unless they form an explicit narrative or ritual motif; they are neither legendary personas nor production mythology records.
